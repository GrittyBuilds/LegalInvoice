Billing & Trust for Law Firms
Overview · September 2026

Bill to the tenth.
Reconcile to the cent.

Tenth is billing and trust accounting for a small law firm: time and fee capture, matter-level discounting, drafts and write-downs, invoices that show the whole account, an IOLTA ledger that can't be confused with operating money, and A/R that answers the question. It runs from a single file on the firm's own machine — no server, no account, no data leaving the office.

Matters, rates and discountsAny number of matters per client, each with its own file number, rate and discount profile — flat, tiered, or none.
Tiered discounts that print20% within ten days, 10% by month end. The schedule appears on the invoice with deadlines and resulting totals, and can be honoured late by choice.
Trust kept visibly separatePer-client IOLTA ledger with deposits, applications and refunds; trust figures carry their own colour everywhere they appear.
Running balances that freezePrevious balance is captured as of the invoice date, so a reprint shows what was owed then.
Drafts, write-downs, then issueInvoices start outside A/R. Write down lines, adjust, finalise. Reopen an issued invoice if you must.
A/R, collections, CSVAging by client, revenue by period, one-click reminders, and exports for the accountant.
Several billing entitiesEach with its own letterhead, numbering, trust account, terms, tax and late fees.
Payments and creditsAllocate a payment across open invoices or auto-apply to oldest; unallocated amounts sit as credit.
A finished invoice — the firm's letterhead, not ours
