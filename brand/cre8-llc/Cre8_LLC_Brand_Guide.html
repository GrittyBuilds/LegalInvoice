Brand Guidelines

The joint legal & regulatory consulting entity of Seyburn Law and Aronoff Law, serving DeHydr8 and its affiliated companies. A clean, science-forward identity that bridges into DeHydr8's world — deep indigo with a signal-green-to-teal gradient.

Usage

Do
  • Keep the "8" in the green→teal gradient — it's the brand signature.
  • Give the logo clear space equal to the monogram height.
  • Use the reverse lockup on indigo or dark backgrounds.
  • Use the C8 monogram alone for avatars and favicons.
Don't
  • Recolor the gradient or flatten the "8" to a solid color.
  • Stretch, rotate, or crowd the lockup.
  • Set the wordmark in another typeface.
  • Place the light logo on a busy or low-contrast background.
